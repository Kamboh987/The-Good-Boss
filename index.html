Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 1-course-per-week phased structure work?
The 12-week program is divided into three 4-week phases. Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4) is fixed on the Pyramid Base (Life and Self-Development). Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8) focuses on Wellness and Nutrition where you choose 1 specialized elective from our 20 curated tracks. Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12) is fixed on the Pyramid Top Tier (Leadership and Organizational Development). Each week unlocks one intensive course module with practical deliverables.
How do returning clients start directly at Phase 2 for $2,000?
If you have previously graduated from Phase 1 or completed a verified The Good Boss workshop, you are eligible for the \$2,000 Returning Client Track. You bypass Phase 1 and immediately dive into your chosen Phase 2 elective and Phase 3 capstone for an 8-week accelerated journey.
What is covered under the $35,000 Corporate Intervention?
Our \$35,000 corporate package is a comprehensive 90-day intervention covering the four vital corporate pillars: Leadership Development (bench grooming and C-suite cohesion), Corporate Turnarounds (operational and cultural triage), Change Maxing (high-velocity change management without fatigue), and Growth Acceleration (go-to-market and scaling engines). It includes executive coaching for up to 25 leaders, war-room playbooks, and tangible metric milestones.
